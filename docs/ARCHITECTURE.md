## 1. Philosophy & Design Principles

- **Presence Over Persona:** The system privileges real-time, verified human presence over static identities or “accounts.”
- **Agent Quorum Security:** Every identity operation (creation, validation, fusion) is distributed among a ritual of agents—no single point of failure or trust.
- **Radical Transparency:** All protocol actions are logged. Identities are ephemeral; audit trails are permanent.
- **Learning Environment:** The codebase, like the protocol, is designed for onboarding and rapid skill acquisition. PARA’s mission: environments that grow people, not just software.
